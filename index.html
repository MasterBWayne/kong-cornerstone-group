Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the most common tenant questions.

How do I pay rent? ﹢

Rent is due on the 1st of each month. Payment instructions are included in your lease agreement. If you have questions about payment methods, submit a General Inquiry through the Tenant Portal above.

How do I submit a maintenance request? ﹢

Use the Tenant Portal on this page. Select "Maintenance Request," fill out the form with as much detail as possible, and submit. You'll receive a confirmation and we'll follow up within 48 hours. Urgent issues are prioritized immediately.

What counts as a maintenance emergency? ﹢

Emergencies include: no heat in winter, flooding or active leaks, gas smell, no running water, broken locks or security issues. For emergencies, select "Urgent / Emergency" in the form and we will respond as soon as possible.

How long will maintenance take? ﹢

Urgent issues are addressed immediately. Standard requests are typically handled within 2–5 business days depending on the nature of the repair. We will keep you updated on the status.

How do I renew my lease? ﹢

We will reach out to you 60–90 days before your lease expires. If you'd like to discuss renewal earlier, submit a General Inquiry through the portal and we'll be in touch.

What is your noise policy? ﹢

Quiet hours are 10pm–8am. Excessive noise that disturbs other residents is a lease violation. If you have a noise complaint, please submit it through the Tenant Portal and we will address it.

Can I have a pet? ﹢

Pet policies vary by unit. Please refer to your lease agreement or submit a General Inquiry to ask about your specific unit.

What happens if I pay rent late? ﹢

Late fees may apply as specified in your lease. If you are experiencing a hardship, please reach out through the Tenant Portal before the due date — we prefer to work with tenants proactively.

How do I report a building safety issue? ﹢

Submit a Complaint through the Tenant Portal and select "Safety / Security Concern." For immediate danger, call 911 first, then notify us.

How do I contact management? ﹢

Use the Tenant Portal on this page for all requests. For general questions, email management@kongcornerstonegroup.com. We respond within 1–2 business days.
