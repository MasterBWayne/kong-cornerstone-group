About

A residential property management company serving New York City.

Kong Cornerstone Group manages residential rental properties across New York City, with active operations in Queens and the Bronx. We focus on dependable day-to-day management, clear communication with residents, and maintaining the condition and compliance of each property we oversee.

We operate as a private management company — prioritizing stability, responsible stewardship, and the straightforward delivery of housing services.

What We Do

Property management, handled with care.

Day-to-Day Operations

Routine maintenance coordination, vendor oversight, and ongoing property upkeep to keep buildings running smoothly.

Resident Relations

Compliance & Reporting

Keeping properties current with applicable local, state, and federal housing regulations and reporting requirements.

Long-Term Stewardship

A patient, long-term approach to property oversight — prioritizing building integrity and resident stability over short-term gains.
