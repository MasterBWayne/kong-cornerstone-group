About the Company

A private, owner-operated residential real estate firm serving New York City communities.

Kong Cornerstone Group acquires, manages, and maintains residential rental properties with a focus on quality housing and responsible ownership. We take a long-term, community-minded approach to property management across our New York City portfolio.

As a privately held company, we maintain direct operational control over our properties — providing stable, well-maintained homes for our residents and consistent stewardship of our assets.
